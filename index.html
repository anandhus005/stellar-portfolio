Curious by nature. A builder by choice.

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering student from Kerala, interested in software development, creative technology, AI and building useful digital products. I build software people can use or play with.

My work follows two tracks. The first is full-stack web apps with Django and Spring Boot on SQL or MongoDB databases: Campus Exchange and the Agriculture and Library management systems. The second is creative coding: PixelForge, Octopus Depths and Tick Tick explore generative art, scroll-driven animation and playful interaction.

I learn by building something real, shipping it to Vercel and fixing what breaks. Right now I'm going deeper into React and TypeScript, and learning DevOps and cloud deployment so what I ship is easier to run and maintain.
